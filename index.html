Takeaway

Summary

  • Space Black leads on both models at 60% (14-inch) and 63% (16-inch). On average, 46% of customers asking for the latest MacBook Pro are ready to choose touchscreen.
  • The 14-inch leans to the M6 chip (46%), and the 16-inch to the M5 Pro (66%). 1TB is the top capacity on both.
  • Three in four customers are upgraders. M2 Pro is the most cited prior model, and Lenovo leads the switch-from brands.
  • Creative work by touch is the top potential use on both models. About half of customers ask about affordability options.
  • Strong performance and the lighter design with touchscreen are the top features. Fingerprints and app support are the main objections, and price comes up in the comments from half of UAE attended stores.
